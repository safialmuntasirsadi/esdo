About the Organization

Empowering Communities Since 1988

Environmental and Social Development Organization (ESDO) is a leading NGO working across 56 districts of Bangladesh, reaching over 15.73 million beneficiaries through sustainable development initiatives.

Our eco-tourism project follows the eco-village model — integrating community participation, environmental conservation, and economic empowerment to create sustainable tourism destinations in northern Bangladesh.

Aligned with the UN Sustainable Development Goals, we focus on SDGs 1, 4, 5, 8, 12, 13, 15, and 17 — building tourism that lifts communities out of poverty while protecting our natural heritage.

Where Nature Meets Culture

Explore Northern Bangladesh

Untouched landscapes, rich heritage, and warm hospitality await in the northern districts of Bangladesh.

Thakurgaon

Ramrai Dighi & Surjapuri Heritage

Explore the historic Ramrai Dighi, a centuries-old pond surrounded by lush greenery, and visit the legendary Surjapuri Mango Tree — a living monument of the region's agricultural heritage.

Heritage Nature Culture
Panchagarh

Banglabandha Zero Point & Tea Estates

Stand at the northernmost tip of Bangladesh at Banglabandha Zero Point, explore the Kazi & Kazi organic tea estates, and discover the unique Rocks Museum — a testament to geological wonders.

Adventure Tea Gardens Geology
Dinajpur

Kantaji Temple & Rural Eco-Villages

Visit the magnificent Kantaji Terracotta Temple, one of the finest examples of Hindu temple architecture in South Asia, and experience life in ESDO's model eco-villages.

Architecture Community History

Our Approach

Sustainable Tourism, Lasting Impact

Every initiative is designed to benefit communities while preserving the environment for future generations.

🌱

Environmental Conservation

Protecting biodiversity, promoting organic practices, and minimizing the ecological footprint of tourism activities in sensitive ecosystems.

👩‍👩‍👧

Community Empowerment

Training local communities as tourism entrepreneurs — from homestay management to guided tours — creating sustainable livelihoods.

📚

Cultural Preservation

Documenting and celebrating indigenous traditions, crafts, and heritage sites to keep cultural identity alive for future generations.

💰

Economic Growth

Channeling tourism revenue directly into local economies through community-based tourism enterprises and fair-trade practices.

♻

Responsible Travel

Educating visitors on sustainable practices, waste management, and respectful engagement with local communities and ecosystems.

🤝

Partnership & Collaboration

Working with government bodies, international organizations, and local stakeholders to build a scalable eco-tourism framework.
