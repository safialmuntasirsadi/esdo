**Story Angle:** A 42-acre village pond in Thakurgaon attracts birds from Siberia, Mongolia, Tibet, and Serbia every winter — including Greater Flamingos that surprised wildlife officials. **Visual Hook:** Dawn mist over the lake with thousands of white herons and pink flamingos against golden sunrise water.

**Best Season:** December–February

- surjapuri mango tree

**Long-tail Keywords:**
- "oldest mango tree in bangladesh"
- "surjapuri mango tree baliadangi how to visit"

- "heritage trees of northern bangladesh"

**Story Angle:** A single living organism, 200+ years old, with a canopy covering 0.5 acres and 19 main branches so thick they've formed secondary trunks. You literally walk *inside* this tree. Nobody knows exactly who planted it — only oral history traces it back through generations. It's the most massive mango tree in South Asia, and it's sitting quietly in a border village with no heritage protection.

**Visual Hook:** Drone shot showing a tree the size of a building; ground-level shot of a person standing beneath branches that form a green cathedral ceiling.

**Best Season:** Year-round (peak visual: June–August during mango season)

**Story Angle:** Every country has an edge. Bangladesh's edge is at Banglabandha Zero Point in Tetulia, where the flag-lowering ceremony plays out daily between BSF and BGB — a quieter, more intimate version of the famous Wagah border ceremony. You can stand at the concrete marker and say: this is where my country ends. Nepal is 30 km away, Bhutan and India are right there, and you're at the crossroads of four nations.

**Visual Hook:** The zero-point monument against flat northern plains at sunset, the Indian flag visible meters away, the dramatic sky of a border crossing.

## Blog #6: Panchagarh Rocks Museum — Bangladesh's Only Rock Museum
**Title:** "The Museum of Ancient Rocks in a Country Made of Mud: Panchagarh's Geological Secret"

**Target Keywords:**
- panchagarh rocks museum
- rock museum bangladesh
- petrified wood bangladesh
- panchagarh women's college museum
- geology tourism bangladesh

**Long-tail Keywords:**
- "only rock museum in bangladesh"
- "panchagarh museum ancient stones"
- "300 year old boat panchagarh"
- "himalayan rocks in bangladesh museum"

**Story Angle:** Bangladesh is a delta — alluvial silt, river deposits, mud. But in Panchagarh, at the country's northernmost edge, there's a museum of ancient rocks, petrified wood, volcanic stones, and 300-year-old boats. Why? Because this is where the Himalayan rock cycle meets the Bengal basin. Stones from Tibet, Bhutan, and Darjeeling have traveled thousands of kilometers by river to end up in a college campus museum in the smallest district of Bangladesh.

**Visual Hook:** Petrified wood with preserved grain patterns, massive ancient boulders arranged in a garden, the surreal juxtaposition of geological specimens against a Bangladeshi college backdrop.

**Best Season:** Year-round (indoor/outdoor)

## Blog #7: Kanchenjunga from Tetulia — The Himalayan View from Bangladesh
**Title:** "I Saw the World's Third-Highest Mountain From Bangladesh (And Nobody Believed Me)"

**Target Keywords:**
- kanchenjunga view from bangladesh
- himalaya from tetulia
- tetulia dak bungalow
- mountain view panchagarh
- everest visible from bangladesh
- kanchenjunga bangladesh october november

**Long-tail Keywords:**
- "can you see himalayas from bangladesh"
- "kanchenjunga view from tetulia best time"
- "mountain viewing spots northern bangladesh"
- "everest visible from bangladesh november"

**Story Angle:** Ask any Bangladeshi if you can see the Himalayas from Bangladesh, and they'll say no. But from late October to mid-November, at dawn, from Tetulia Dak Bungalow — Mount Kanchenjunga (8,586m) rises 157 km away above the flat northern plains. In November–December, Everest appears too. This is a 2-3 week window when Bangladesh becomes a mountain-viewing country, and most people don't know it exists.

**Visual Hook:** A massive white snow peak rising above flat green tea gardens and rice paddies at dawn — a juxtaposition so improbable that the photos look edited.

**Best Season:** October–mid-November (Kanchenjunga), November–December (Everest). Early morning/dawn only.

**Story Angle:** A zamindar palace built in 1893 on landholdings dating back to 1400 CE is crumbling to dust. Every year, there's less of the palace left to see.

## Blog #9: Tangon River & Senua Bridge — The River and the Broken Bridge
**Title:** "The River That Holds Up a District and the Bridge That Couldn't: Thakurgaon's Untold Story"

**Target Keywords:**
- tangon river thakurgaon
- senua river bridge
- thakurgaon river tourism
- bailey bridge thakurgaon
- rural bangladesh infrastructure
- thakurgaon nature spots

**Long-tail Keywords:**
- "tangon river thakurgaon tourist spot"
- "senua bailey bridge collapse story"
- "river tourism northern bangladesh"
- "peaceful river walks thakurgaon"

**Story Angle:** The Tangon River is called "the heart of Thakurgaon" by locals — it feeds the agriculture, the fisheries, the daily life of thousands. The Senua Bailey Bridge collapsed in 2017 when a coal truck crossed it, and for years it stayed broken. Locals paid 10 taka to cross on makeshift bamboo bridges. This isn't just a river story — it's a story about what rural Bangladesh depends on and what happens when the infrastructure fails.

**Visual Hook:** Golden reflections on the Tangon at sunset, the serpentine Senua River winding through flat agricultural land, the rusted steel truss of the collapsed Bailey Bridge against the rural backdrop.

**Best Season:** Year-round (best light: late afternoon)
